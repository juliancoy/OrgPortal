Keep the layers distinct

Four different objects.
Four different questions.

A medical specialty, a diagnosis code, a service code, and a payment rule may all describe the same episode of care—but they are not interchangeable.

Field Neurology

Organizes people, knowledge, research, and clinical practice.

Diagnosis family ICD-10-CM G00–G99

Classifies documented diseases of the nervous system.

Service CPT / HCPCS / PCS

Reports what was performed, furnished, tested, or administered.

Payer rule Maryland Medicaid

Applies coverage, billing edits, fee schedules, and contracts.

Clinical coding & payment

A claim moves through
several vocabularies.

“Medicaid charge code” is not one universal taxonomy. A claim combines documented diagnoses, services, claim context, and Maryland-specific payment policy. A hospital may also maintain its own local charge codes.

≠
Hospital charge ≠ Medicaid payment

A chargemaster entry is a provider’s local accounting object. Standard claim codes and payer rules determine how that line is interpreted and adjudicated.
